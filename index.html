Nordic Power Lab

Live 30-minute-to-3-hour forecasts of the Danish imbalance price, built from open data. That price sets what wind farms, batteries and traders pay for missing their schedule, so knowing its direction early lets them adjust before it is settled. Every claim is written down before its test; the money value is being measured in a live test that runs until February 2027.

Hedge-fund vacancy monitor

A Telegram channel with almost 2,000 subscribers and a companion bot. It watches the career pages of about 100 hedge funds, prop shops and trading firms, runs every new role through an agentic processing pipeline and publishes it as it appears.
